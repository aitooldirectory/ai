# ai
Curated list of top AI tool directories

This curated list has been updated and verified for 2025. Several directories have significantly expanded their catalogs, while new specialized platforms have emerged.

## 🧭 Top AI Tool Directories

Discover, explore, and submit to the best AI tool directories—perfect for keeping up with the latest in AI tools, frameworks, and solutions.


### 🌍 Web-Based Directories

- **Aixploria.com** – Diverse collection with detailed tool profiles and collaborative content.
- **OpenFuture.ai** – Wide, fast searchable tool library and strong community feedback.

- **[There’s an AI For That](https://theresanaiforthat.com/)** – Currently the largest AI directory in 2025, hosting over 97,000 tools for every possible use case.
- **[Toolify.ai](https://www.toolify.ai/)** – A dominant platform in 2025, listing over 26,000 tools across 450+ categories with advanced trend tracking.
- **[Futurepedia](https://www.futurepedia.io/)** – Now features over 5,500 curated tools with verified reviews, daily updates, and specialized guides for professionals.
- **[Aixploria](https://www.aixploria.com/)** – Offers a massive collection of 5,000+ tools categorized by specific professions and creative industries.
- **[OpenTools.ai](https://opentools.ai/)** – Known for its community-driven Q&A sessions and detailed step-by-step workflow guides for business AI.
- **[FutureTools](https://www.futuretools.io/)** – Curated by Matt Wolfe, this directory focuses on high-quality, frequently used tools with a strong emphasis on automation and productivity.

### 🆕 New for 2025: Specialized & Marketplace Directories

- **[Spotsaas](https://www.spotsaas.com/)** – A fast-growing software marketplace with over 40,000 products and 1 million+ reviews, bridging the gap between standard SaaS and AI.
- **[TechPilot AI](https://techpilot.ai/)** – Focuses on unbiased, real-world testing of tools specifically for business workflows and decision-making.
- **[Find An AI Agent](https://aiagentstore.ai/)** – A new 2025 niche directory specifically for Autonomous AI Agents and agentic search platforms.
- **[GrabOn AI](https://www.grabon.in/ai/)** – A massive daily-updated directory that includes discount codes and deals for premium AI subscriptions.
